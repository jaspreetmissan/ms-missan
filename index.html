👋 About Me

📱 Class Communication

Rooms: messages from me

I use Rooms for class messages. It's free and built into the EUSD app (also works on the web).

  1. Open the invite text/email or the EUSD app and tap Rooms.
  2. Enter the phone or email the school has on file.
  3. Choose "Sign in with code" and enter the code you receive.
  4. Turn on notifications so you never miss a message.
